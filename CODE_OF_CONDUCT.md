# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Conduit contributors, maintainers, and participants commit to a respectful, harassment-free community regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or technical preference.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, security embargoes, and differing experience levels.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes occur.
- Prioritize the safety and health of the project community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, trolling, doxxing, publishing private communication, deliberate security harm, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate is not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository owner's GitHub profile contact. Do not use vulnerability reports for ordinary conduct disputes. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy where possible and may remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or permanently ban participants based on context and impact.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents Conduit. Enforcement decisions should be fair, documented privately, and avoid conflicts of interest.
